## CapCut desktop: step by step

1. **New project.** Click the ratio button above the preview and choose **9:16**.
2. **Import.** Drag the eight PNGs into the media panel, then onto the timeline in order 01 → 07, then 09 (or 08).
3. **Set lengths.** Click each clip and drag its right edge: 1.5, 1.5, 1.5, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2.5 seconds (total 15 s).
4. **Add music.** Audio tab → pick a track → drag it under the pictures. Trim it to 15 s.
5. **Cut on the beat.** Right-click the music → *Beat detection* (or *Auto beat*). Nudge each picture's edge onto a beat marker. Put the biggest hit on picture 4.
6. **Animate.** Click a clip → **Animation** (right panel) → **In** → pick the effect from the table.
7. **Transitions (optional).** Use quick *Zoom* or *Swipe* (0.2–0.3 s). A *Flash* or *Glitch* transition into picture 4 makes the switch to the social half stand out.
8. **Sound effects (optional).** Audio → Sound effects: a "whoosh" on slides, a "ding" on picture 3 (correct answer), a "boxing bell" on picture 5 (battle).
9. **Voiceover (optional).** Click the microphone icon above the timeline and record the line above. Then Text → *Auto captions*.
10. **Export.** 1080p, 30 fps, MP4.
