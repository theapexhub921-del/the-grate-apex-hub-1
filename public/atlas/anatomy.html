    Credits and licence

    Anatomy models: the Z-Anatomy project, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. Z-Anatomy is derived from BodyParts3D.

    Changes made to the models: converted from FBX to glTF (GLB) and compressed with Meshopt; label and leader-line helper meshes and unused texture coordinates removed; a lighter copy for phones simplified to about half the triangles; landmark ownership data added. Colours and lighting are applied by this viewer. The modified models are shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.

    Vagina and ligaments, female reproductive organs, breasts: HuBMAP Human Reference Atlas 3D reference objects (Visible Human Female, National Library of Medicine), licensed CC BY 4.0. Positioned and renamed for this viewer.

    Moving around without the buttons

    • Drag to turn the model
    • Scroll to zoom toward the pointer
    • Right-drag, or Shift + drag, to move it
    • Double-click a structure to zoom to it; double-click empty space to reset
    • Arrow keys turn, Shift + arrows move, + and - zoom
    • Drag with one finger to turn the model
    • Pinch to zoom toward your fingers
    • Drag with two fingers to move it
    • Double-tap a structure to zoom to it; double-tap empty space to reset
